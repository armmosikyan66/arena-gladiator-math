# Keno Xtreme Hard — picks and multipliers

Stake Keno Xtreme **Hard** HUD (docx: Degen). Source:
[[sources/keno-xtreme-hard-hud]] (screenshots 2026-09-01). Board: pool 40,
draw 10, picks 1–10. Bottom-row `Nx` is **hits**. luma-keno analogue is Off
**`high`**. How the shipped multipliers are generated:
[[concepts/keno-paytable-generation]] Algorithm A.

Do not paste picks 2–5 HUD tops (player-favor; pick 3 is ~614% RTP at 500×).
Picks 5–8 sit on the std-18.3 envelope (HIGH, not EXTREME): **900 / 2200 /
5500 / 6100**. Picks 9–10 keep the HUD jackpots (**40,000 / 50,000**).
Pick 10 body is a geometric leftover-fill onto **50,000×**, not the packed
Degen 1.5 / 3 / 90 cliff.
